This is a Card game, if you couldnt tell.

You need to play out your card to damage and finially defeat your enemy.

Different enemies have different types of attacks, some even have a ability or something unique.

But don't worry you can see all of the informations about your enemy on their card.

If you die, you lose one of your hearts and Will be sent back to the previous enemy level.

You can even customize what music you play.

If you really wanna get into tactics you can sell your cards. Sell cause you can buy more in the shop,

You can even buy upgrade your cards till level 9! It's only good to buy damage cards though, cause the other cards dont get buffs from level ups,

so if you get those in the shop... well, unlucky.

You can get card upgrades from the shop BEFORE buying them, thats how it works in this game.

So build a good deck (max 10, min 3 cards) and defeat all your enemys.

Oh and what if you lose all your hearts and die? Well, then you lose your save and your game closes.

There is a console in the game, you can find the commands for it in the game files.
Oh and if your game is buggy a little bit (cards arent on place or i cant play out a card) type in the console "game fix" and it will be fixed.
